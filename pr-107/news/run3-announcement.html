GF180MCU Run 3 is Here!

01 Aug 2026 By Tim 'mithro' Ansell

Hey folks,

It’s that time again – Run 3 is open for submission.

What’s new?

We’re now able to offer 1,000 dies for as little as $2,000 – that’s $2/die. This is achievable thanks to our newest slot offering: the 0.5x0.5 quarter slot. This tiny slot packs a punch with 4.9 mm² of silicon allowing you to tapeout test structures, analog circuits, or compact digital designs without paying for silicon you do not need.

Quarter slot overlaid on a full slot die for size comparison

Detailed comparisons between the different slot sizes can be seen at mithro.github.io/gf180mcu-project-template/. If you’re not sure what size would suit you best, don’t hesitate to reach out to us or our community - see the Get Involved section.

Behind the scenes, there have been various toolchain improvements and bugfixes to make the processes a lot smoother. The submission platform now catches more issues, earlier and faster. Stricter DRCs allow you to be confident in your tapeout.

With Run 2 closed, there’s another batch of open source designs available for you to study, adapt and build upon for your own tapeout. Projects vary from simple test structures to Linux-capable SoCs, covering digital, analog and mixed-signal approaches.

Schedule

Date Milestone
1 August 2026 Campaign opens on Crowd Supply.
15 December 2026 Slot purchase deadline.

Q2 2027 Bare dies and packaged parts shipped to customers.


Get Involved

If you are interested in Run 3, or if you want to share it with someone who the previous runs, here is how to stay in the loop:

  • Run 3 Launch Page: Visit our Crowd Supply page for more information about Run 3 and how you can get started
  • Discord: wafer.space/discord
  • Matrix: wafer.space/matrix

Thank You

To our community and the wider open source silicon ecosystem – thank you. Your help in backing these runs, testing your designs, contributing patches to the tools and providing feedback about anything and everything has been monumental in helping shape all of our runs, and Run 3 is no different. You help us make Run 3 the best it can be, and we hope to keep bringing opportunities for future tapeouts. Thank you for being a part of this - see you on the next tapeout.

Founder

wafer.space was founded by Tim ‘mithro’ Ansell, a pioneer in open-source silicon and a dedicated advocate for accessible chip design. With extensive expertise spanning over two decades in open-source hardware and software, Tim has significantly influenced the development and adoption of open-source FPGA toolchains and Electronic Design Automation (EDA) software. He has played instrumental roles in various community-driven projects, including OpenROAD, the FOSSi Foundation, and Google’s open-source initiatives such as SkyWater’s open PDK and the Open MPW program.

Following the collapse of Efabless in 2025, Tim saw an urgent need to rebuild trust and expand opportunities within the silicon community. Leveraging his robust industry connections, community management experience, and deep technical expertise, he established wafer.space with the mission to create a transparent, community-oriented silicon pooling service.

Under Tim’s leadership, wafer.space is committed to breaking down barriers to custom silicon fabrication, fostering collaboration, and driving forward the democratization of semiconductor technology, enabling engineers, startups, and researchers globally to innovate freely and efficiently.
