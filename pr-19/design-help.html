D. Mitch Bailey#

Physical Verification Support for GF180MCU Designs

Mitch provides physical verification support for open-source GF180MCU projects, focused on diagnosing and resolving LVS/ERC failures and helping teams tapeout with confidence.

DJ Semiconductor Services#

Die Imaging, Decapsulation & Deprocessing

DJ Semiconductor Services is the EU-based imaging lab of Dmitry “InfoSecDJ” Janushkevich, offering semiconductor decapsulation, deprocessing, and imaging. If you want to see what actually came back from the fab — for debugging, documentation, teaching, or simply a beautiful picture of your chip — Dmitry can photograph it.

See it on wafer.space silicon#

Dmitry photographed the fabricated dies from GF180MCU Run 1 (the picture here is his micrograph of the wafer.space logo die). Every image is a stitched panorama of the whole die, published on the run pages:

  • All Run 1 die photographs — 21 designs
  • KIAN — KianV, a 32-bit RISC-V Linux SoC
  • MOLE — FABulous FPGA
  • RBOY — RISCBoy-180
  • GD03 — Racquet, a 23 core SoC
  • TQVA — TinyQV, a crowdsourced RISC-V SoC
  • MOSB — MOSbiusV3
  • WSLG — the wafer.space logo die

The full-resolution files are free to download, at 200 to 500 MB per die.

Services#

  • Die imaging: optical microscopy panoramas of effectively unlimited size, at up to 1000x magnification, of a whole die or a region of interest
  • Decapsulation: getting the die out of ceramic and epoxy (DIP, QFP, DFN, simple BGA) packages
  • Deprocessing: removing the metal layers that hide the features underneath
  • Delivery: a single image file, or a web-based deep-zoom dataset for easy inspection

Decapsulation and deprocessing can destroy the sample, so plan on sending several. The optical setup is at its best on process nodes down to about 350nm; the Run 1 photographs show what to expect on GF180MCU.

  • Or use the contact form at infosecdj.net

Outerfab#

Post-fab Services for Small Silicon Batches

Outerfab turns bare silicon into boards you can work with, from a first prototype to a small production batch. Based in Shenzhen, it supports open-silicon designers, startups and research groups working with die from wafer.space and other shuttles.

Services#

  • PCB design and review: Breakout boards, bonding schematics, pad maps and panelisation for chip-on-board assembly.
  • Chip-on-board wire bonding: Die attach, aluminium wedge or gold thermosonic bonding, with colourless or black hard epoxy glob-top.
  • Electrical testing: Checks for open connections and shorts, with coverage, expected readings and reporting agreed for each job.
  • Wafer dicing and division: Backgrinding, dicing and sorting die into waffle packs through processing partners in Asia.
  • Known-good die sourcing: Sourcing off-the-shelf parts as bare die where suppliers offer them.
  • Kitting and fulfilment: Board QC, packing, kitting and shipping to customers or fulfilment partners.
  • Crowd Supply launch support: Pre-launch communication, campaign setup and management, and production and delivery coordination.

Partcl#

GPU-Accelerated Digital Implementation & Timing

Partcl builds Boson, a GPU-native digital implementation engine covering synthesis, placement, clock tree synthesis, and static timing analysis. By running the entire flow on GPUs, Boson turns full-chip iterations that traditionally take hours into minutes.

For GF180MCU shuttle projects, that speed changes how you design:

  • Explore before you commit: run many architecture, floorplan, and constraint experiments before the shuttle deadline instead of one or two
  • Timing confidence before tapeout: fast timing analysis with parasitics lets you verify your design closes timing before committing to a shuttle slot
  • Works alongside your existing flow: use Boson for rapid QoR and timing exploration while signing off with the open-source flow you already trust

Partcl’s team combines EDA and GPU-computing backgrounds, and offers direct support for teams pushing timing or turnaround limits on their designs.

Precision Innovations#

OpenROAD Development & Commercial Support

Precision Innovations is the primary developer of OpenROAD™, the open-source RTL-to-GDS flow widely used for digital designs on GF180MCU. Founded in 2019 by experienced EDA engineers with over 30 years of industry expertise, their unique position as the tool developers—not just users—enables capabilities far beyond what’s available in the standard open-source version.

When designing with GF180MCU, nearly everyone relies on OpenROAD for their digital implementation flow. Precision Innovations offers commercial support and enhancements that can be critical when:

  • OpenROAD bugs block your design: Direct access to the development team means bugs can be fixed in the tools themselves, not worked around
  • Advanced optimization is needed: Expert assistance using ML-powered optimization tools to achieve better power, performance, and area (PPA) results
  • Custom features are required: New functionality can be developed specifically for your project’s needs

OpenROAD, the tool they develop, has been used for thousands of successful tapeouts on GF180MCU and SKY130 through Google-sponsored shuttles and Efabless programs. Their deep knowledge of OpenROAD’s capabilities and common challenges enables them to solve complex physical design issues including power delivery network construction, routing congestion, density rule violations, and antenna effects.

Precision Innovations provides several engagement models:

  • Commercial Support: Expert assistance from the OpenROAD development team for production designs
  • Custom Development: Tool enhancements, flow modifications, and feature additions tailored to specific requirements
  • Training & Education: Professional courses taught through UCSC Extension by Precision’s engineering leaders
  • Rapid Turnaround: Automated design flows capable of RTL-to-GDS implementation in under 24 hours

As the “RedHat of OpenROAD,” Precision Innovations bridges the gap between open-source accessibility and production-ready reliability. Their team contributes extensively to the open-source project while offering commercial extensions and support for teams that need guaranteed results on GF180MCU designs.

For teams pushing the boundaries of what’s possible with OpenROAD on GF180MCU, Precision Innovations offers the expertise and direct tool access that can make the difference between a successful tapeout and project delays.
